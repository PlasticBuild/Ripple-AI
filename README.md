APK download releases retain their APK-only attachments. Separate source releases contain the complete source archive, matching manifest and original validation/evidence files. The source releases are marked as prereleases and excluded from the app's automatic update feed.

Extract the complete archive and follow its rebuild instructions. Retain its licenses, notices and stated native rebuild/equivalence limitations. Signing keys, credentials, APKs and private phone evidence are excluded from this source migration. Later private development and experimental NPU161 work are not included.
